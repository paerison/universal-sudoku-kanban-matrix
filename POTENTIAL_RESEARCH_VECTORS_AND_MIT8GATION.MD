## Section Supplement: Potential Research Vectors and Mitigation Strategies

The rigid operational constraint engine of the Universal Sudoku Kanban Matrix (USKM) addresses throughput volatility but introduces significant cognitive-psychological trade-offs. Forcing non-linear cognitive work into deterministic grid configurations can trigger acute system-level pathologies, specifically Divergent Atrophy and Operational Neurosis. To transition the USKM from a closed algorithmic system into an adaptive cognitive tool, future research must validate structural mitigation mechanisms that balance boundary control with cognitive flexibility.

### Potential Research Vectors

#### 1. Quantifying Cognitive Switching Costs and Neurological Over-Allocation
Future empirical validation must measure the neurocognitive load imposed by constant algorithmic validation. While the matrix minimizes administrative noise, the cognitive processing required to maintain alignment with intersecting spatial rule blocks may exhaust executive functioning resources. Research should leverage functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) and eye-tracking metrics to monitor cognitive load when users transition between spatial task movement and multi-axis mathematical verification. 

#### 2. Longitudinal Field Studies on Tactical Evasion and Systemic Dropouts
A critical research avenue involves tracking user interaction patterns over extended operational horizons. Longitudinal studies should examine the precise operational inflection points where users abandon the matrix rules in favor of unstructured workarounds. Measuring the time delta between the onset of high-density workflow congestion and the initialization of tracking evasion behaviors will provide data-driven thresholds for when system boundaries transition from protective structures into administrative barriers.
